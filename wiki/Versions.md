The modern ports use `update/streams-2.17.1-<loader>-<minecraft>` branches. They have separate JARs and Java/dependency requirements. Choose by the actual Minecraft and loader metadata, and keep clients and server aligned.

Old Flow Wands retain their item identity and become Calibrators in Water Engineering. Existing controller settings migrate into Profile A; legacy wand edits remain removable.
